# Privacy

Lumik stores your account identifier, uploaded documents, extracted information, groups, and chat history to provide document organization and search.

## Processing

Documents are stored privately. Document content may be sent to configured AI providers to extract information and answer your requests.

## Deletion

You can request account deletion in Account settings and cancel within seven days. After that period your Lumik data is removed and stored files are queued for deletion. Your RxLab sign-in account is separate.
